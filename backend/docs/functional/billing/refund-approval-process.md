# Refund approval process

## Who approves
Refunds up to 200 EUR are approved automatically. Refunds above 200 EUR need approval from a billing team lead. Refunds above 2,000 EUR need finance approval as well.

## Customer communication
The customer receives a confirmation email when the refund is approved and a second email when funds are sent. Funds normally arrive within 3 to 5 working days.
